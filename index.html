Why is the Gradient at 90°? (Orthogonality Theorem)

Along the level set curve f(x) = c, the value is constant, so directional derivative is 0. By chain rule, ∇f · T = 0, proving that the gradient vector ∇f is strictly perpendicular (at 90 degrees) to the tangent line of the circle/level curve!
